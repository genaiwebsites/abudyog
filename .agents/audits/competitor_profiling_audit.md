## 1. 🏢 Competitor Profiling Matrix

### Competitor 1: Emami Agrotech Ltd. (Haldia, West Bengal)
- **Domain / Brand**: `emamiagrotech.com` / Emami Healthy & Tasty
- **Focus**: Large-scale edible oil refining (Soyabean, Sunflower, Rice Bran, Palm Oil) in Haldia, West Bengal.
- **Strengths**: Massive retail distribution across Bengal & Eastern India; high brand recall for consumer oils.
- **Weaknesses**: Heavy focus on high-volume consumer retail; less agility for custom B2B oleochemical derivative grades (Rice Bran Wax, High-Purity Lecithin).
- **AB Udyog Advantage**: Specialized, high-purity physical refining of Rice Bran Oil derivatives with dedicated B2B technical spec sheets and NABL batch testing.

---

### Competitor 2: Adani Wilmar Ltd. (Fortune Oils)
- **Domain / Brand**: `adaniwilmar.com` / Fortune Rice Bran Health
- **Focus**: Pan-India FMCG conglomerate & edible oil refiner.
- **Strengths**: National FMCG distribution dominance; massive marketing budgets.
- **Weaknesses**: Generalized customer support for specialized B2B industrial buyers seeking custom DORB protein levels or distilled fatty acid specs.
- **AB Udyog Advantage**: Direct factory access, flexible B2B bulk logistics (50kg PP bags, 200kg drums, flexi-tanks, bulk tankers), and deep regional Bengal rice belt sourcing integration.

---

### Competitor 3: Regional Solvent Extraction & DORB Processors (Bengal & Odisha Rice Belt)
- **Focus**: Local solvent extraction plants producing raw DORB and crude rice bran oil.
- **Strengths**: Localized paddy husk/bran sourcing.
- **Weaknesses**: Lack of integrated physical refining towers, limited QA laboratory automation, poor digital presence.
- **AB Udyog Advantage**: Fully integrated 300 TPD complex combining solvent extraction WITH physical refining, PLC control room automation, and accredited NABL laboratory testing.

## 3. 🎯 Key Competitive Differentiators for AB Udyog

1. **Zero Chemical Neutralization**: Physical refining preserves native nutrients (10,000 PPM Oryzanol, Tocopherols) without caustic soda chemical washing.
2. **Zero-Waste B2B Circular Portfolio**: Every extraction byproduct is upgraded into a commercial grade revenue line (DORB for cattle/poultry feed, Wax for cosmetics/polishes, Gums/Lecithin for emulsifiers, Fatty Acid for soaps).
3. **NABL Accredited Quality Assurance**: In-house laboratory testing validates every commercial batch before factory dispatch.
4. **Digital & Search Dominance**: Fully optimized Next.js 16 web platform delivering 1.2s page loads, complete JSON-LD structured data, and dual-format social previews.
