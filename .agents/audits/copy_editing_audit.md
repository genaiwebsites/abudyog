## 1. 🔍 Detailed Seven Sweeps Framework Analysis

### Sweep 1: Clarity & Message Match
- **Finding**: Copy cleanly distinguishes between **B2B Industrial Bulk Derivatives** (DORB, Rice Bran Wax, Gums, Lecithin, Fatty Acid) and **B2C Consumer Edible Oils** (AB Health, Jeevan Rekha).
- **Rule of One**: Every page communicates one single main idea cleanly without overwhelming the reader.

### Sweep 2: Voice & Tone Consistency
- **Tone**: Professional, industrial-grade, quality-first, and customer-centric.
- **Word Choice**: Replaced vague corporate speak with clear, natural language (*"Physically Refined Edible Oils & High-Protein DORB Feed"*).

### Sweep 3: "So What?" Benefit Bridges
- **Example 1**: *"100% Physical Refining (Zero Chemical Neutralization)"*  
  ➡️ *Bridge*: Preserves natural bio-active Oryzanol and Vitamin E while ensuring zero chemical caustic soda residue.
- **Example 2**: *"300 Metric Tons Per Day Processing Capacity"*  
  ➡️ *Bridge*: Guarantees uninterrupted bulk supply and consistent batch-to-batch quality for large industrial manufacturers.

### Sweep 4: "Prove It" Social Proof & Credentials
- **Certifications**: FSSAI, FSSC 22000, ISO 9001, NABL Accredited Laboratory.
- **Visual Proof**: Authentic NABL Quality Assurance Laboratory testing photography and 4K Retina product showcase graphics. 0 fake stock supplement photos.

### Sweep 5: Specificity Upgrade
- **Before**: *"High quality edible oil with good health benefits."*
- **After (Current)**: *"AB Health Physically Refined Rice Bran Oil — Fortified with Vitamins A & D, enriched with 10,000 PPM natural Oryzanol, featuring a high smoke point of 232°C for zero trans fat daily cooking."*

### Sweep 6: Heightened Emotion & Trust
- **B2C Focus**: Pure, heart-smart cooking for family health and daily nutrition.
- **B2B Focus**: Procurement confidence, guaranteed quality specifications, and trusted West Bengal paddy belt sourcing.

### Sweep 7: Zero Risk & Friction Reduction
- **CTA Clarity**: Prominent `Request B2B Quote` CTA button leading to simple contact & wholesale lead capture form.
